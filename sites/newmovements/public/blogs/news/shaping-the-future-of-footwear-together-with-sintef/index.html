Shaping the future of footwear together with SINTEF

Researching materials and systems that can keep footwear in circulation.

Circular footwear requires more than changing a single material. Components, adhesives, manufacturing, collection systems and recycling technologies have to work together.

Collaboration with SINTEF gives New Movements access to scientific expertise and testing methods that help turn broad sustainability goals into specific design decisions.

From ambition to evidence

The research informs prototypes and future production, building knowledge that can improve both durability during use and material recovery afterwards.
