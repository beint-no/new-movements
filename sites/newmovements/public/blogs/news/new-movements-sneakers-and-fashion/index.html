New Movements, sneakers and fashion

How a shoe can take part in fashion without being designed to disappear with a trend.

Sneakers sit at the meeting point of utility, identity and culture. Our approach begins with movement: balanced proportions, adaptable colour and materials selected for repair, care and long-term wear.

We believe relevance can come from clarity rather than constant novelty. Each model is intended to become part of a wardrobe over time, while the circular work around it keeps materials in motion for longer.
