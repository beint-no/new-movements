Finansavisen wrote about us

Norwegian design, family history and the ambitions behind New Movements.

Finansavisen featured New Movements and the journey from a century-old family connection to shoemaking toward an independent contemporary brand.

The coverage explored how design, responsible growth and circular product development come together in the business.
