An update from our founder and designer

Notes on design, responsibility and what comes next.

New Movements began with a simple question: could a modern footwear company make products people genuinely want to keep, while taking responsibility for what happens after years of use?

The work has grown from an Oslo idea into a collaboration between designers, craftspeople, researchers and a community willing to challenge the usual product cycle. Each collection is another practical step—never a claim that the work is finished.

Progress through better systems

Durability, repair, material selection and end-of-life planning all influence the design process. The goal is not novelty for its own sake, but useful improvement that can be measured in the product itself.

The next movement will continue to be built openly, one considered decision at a time.
