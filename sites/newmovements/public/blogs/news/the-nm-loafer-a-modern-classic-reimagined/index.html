The NM Loafer: a modern classic reimagined

A familiar silhouette, redrawn for movement.

The loafer earns its place through versatility. New Movements keeps the recognisable proportions but brings a lighter stance, unisex fit and considered material construction to the form.

Designed in Oslo and made in Portugal, the result moves easily between work, travel and evenings out without becoming tied to one dress code.
