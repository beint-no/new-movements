Opening our flagship store

A physical home for products, people and circular ideas.

The NM Community Center at Steen & Strøm brings New Movements into the heart of Oslo. It is a place to try the complete collection, compare fits and talk directly with the people behind the brand.

The store is also intended as a meeting point. Exhibitions, conversations and collaborations make room for a broader discussion about design, materials and the future of footwear.
