Zen: mindful design for modern movement

A calm silhouette built for active days.

Zen explores balance: cushioning without bulk, technical performance without visual noise, and a form that works across seasons and wardrobes.

The design was refined around how the foot moves through a full day. Its materials and construction are selected to support comfort while remaining consistent with the wider circular ambitions of the collection.
