Our story

Four generations of shoemaking.

New Movements builds on a Norwegian family tradition that began in 1916, when Johnny Evensen set out to make quality goods able to withstand Nordic seasons and last for generations.

A new movement

Today the work continues in Oslo, combining inherited craft knowledge with a circular approach to design, repair and material use.
