Our philosophy

Designed for circularity.

Footwear should be made to live longer. New Movements develops versatile unisex styles with considered construction, replaceable parts where possible and material choices made with the next life of each shoe in mind.

Fewer, better movements

Designed in Oslo and handcrafted in Portugal, every collection balances comfort, longevity and a clear point of view.
